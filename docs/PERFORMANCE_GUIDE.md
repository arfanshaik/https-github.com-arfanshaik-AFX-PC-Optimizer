# AFX Emulator Booster Performance Guide

## Good performance basics

- Keep graphics drivers current.
- Enable Intel VT-x / AMD-V when required by your emulator.
- Use an SSD when possible.
- Avoid assigning all CPU cores or all RAM to the emulator.
- Close heavy apps you do not need.
- Keep the PC cool to reduce thermal throttling.
- Match in-game FPS to your display refresh rate and available hardware.

## Low profile

For older or limited PCs. Start with lower emulator resolution and graphics quality.

## Balanced profile

Best starting point for most 8–16 GB systems.

## High profile

For stronger PCs with sufficient cooling and GPU performance.

## Reminder

No booster can guarantee a specific FPS gain for every computer.
